Web3 Wallet Connector Demo

Learn how to connect your wallet like MetaMask in Web3 apps – this is a static demo. Click the button below to simulate a wallet connection and see how the process works in real applications.

Click the button to connect your wallet

How It Works

1

Install MetaMask

Download the MetaMask browser extension from the official website or your app store to get started with Web3.

2

Click Connect

Visit a Web3 app and click the "Connect Wallet" button to initiate the connection process with your browser.

3

Confirm in Wallet

MetaMask will show a confirmation popup. Review the details and approve the connection to start interacting with the blockchain.
